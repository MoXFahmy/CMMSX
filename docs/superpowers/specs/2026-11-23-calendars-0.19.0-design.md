# CMX 0.19.0 — Calendars, availability and the daily timesheet

Roadmap release 0.19.0, numbered "0.18.0 Calendars and timesheet" on the roadmap before the supplier catalogue was split off.
Status: design for approval.

## Why

Supervisors plan by person, by machine and by contractor. Today the planning board counts each person's weekly working hours
but not holidays, training or sick leave, so it shows capacity that isn't there and nobody is warned when a job lands on a
day someone is away. Contractor visits from 0.17.0 have no date of their own, so they can't be planned. Technicians log hours
order by order and can't see or fill in a whole day in one place. DimoMaint has technician, asset and contractor calendars,
time-slot types and a simplified daily report.

## Decisions taken with the user

- **Extend the planning board** (A) instead of separate calendar pages: one place to plan.
- **Plan by the day** (A): work orders stay dated as today (`PlannedStart` is a date); availability is hours per person per
  day. No clock times on work orders.
- "Go with your picks" for everything else — the calls below marked *(my call)*.

## Goals

1. **Availability**: editable types of time away and extra time; entries per person or team, once or repeating weekly over
   a date range; the board's capacity, colours and warnings use the hours actually available.
2. **Contractor visits** on the board: a visit date on contractor lines and a "by contractor" view where visits are moved
   and their report is entered.
3. **Daily timesheet** on the web and the phone: one person's day, hours and overtime per work order, the day total against
   the hours available; supervisors see a week per person.
4. Smaller board additions: the expected stop on asset rows and "Assign to me" on unassigned work.

## Non-goals (0.19.0)

- Clock times on work orders, an hourly day view, or slots within a day (rejected by decision A).
- Leave requests and approval: absences are entered by planners, not requested by technicians.
- Timesheet approval or locking, payroll export, or overtime rules.
- Contractor visits that span several days as one bar; saved board views (the board's filters stay in its address).
- Import sheets for availability; an availability report.
- Warnings outside the board (the work-order form's person picker, PM generation).

## Anti-goals

- **Capacity that silently disagrees**: the board, the timesheet and the warnings must all compute available hours the same
  way (one function).
- **Hours counted twice or lost**: the timesheet edits the same labour records as the work order's Labour tab; costs, reports
  and the phone see exactly what they see today.
- **Blocking work**: availability warns, never refuses — an emergency can still go to someone on leave.
- **Silent no-ops**: a drag the board can't do (a contractor card onto another company) says why.

## Approaches considered

1. **Extend the planning board (chosen)**: absences, capacity and contractor rows on the existing board; a new Timesheet
   page and phone "My day" page over the existing labour records.
2. **Separate calendar pages** (Technician, Asset, Contractor): closer to DimoMaint, but two places to plan the same work and
   about twice the screens.
3. **Plan by the hour** (start times on work orders, hourly day view, time-range slots): closest to DimoMaint, but changes the
   work-order form, PM generation, the import and the phone for a precision CMX doesn't plan at today.

## What CMX has today (and keeps)

- **Planning board** (`/planning`): week or month, rows by person (plus Unassigned) or by asset, filters site, trade, team and
  "only while stopped", trays for earlier and undated work, upcoming PMs; drag moves a work order's planned start or person
  (`POST /api/planning/move`, `workorders.edit`) with Undo and a warning text in the answer; each person-day shows planned
  hours against their working hours and turns red when over.
- **Working hours** (0.9.0): a weekly pattern per person, else their single site's, else the organization's
  (`WorkingHours.ForEmployee`, `HoursOn`).
- **Teams** (0.9.0) with members; **labour** per work order (`WorkOrderLabor`: person, work date, hours, overtime, notes;
  web `POST/PUT/DELETE /api/work-orders/{id}/labor` with `workorders.execute`; phone action `labor.add`, others' hours with
  `workorders.logForOthers`, last 30 days).
- **Contractor lines** (`WorkOrderContractor`, 0.17.0): company, category, person, planned hours, hours done, done on, rate.
- **PM tasks** have an expected stop (`ExpectedStopHours`); work orders keep their `PmTaskId`.

## Design

### Availability types

- Settings › **Availability types**: code, name, colour, **kind** — *Time away* (reduces the hours available) or *Extra time*
  (adds hours, e.g. an extra shift on a Saturday) — and active. Edited with *Manage settings*.
- Seeded for every organization (migration and new sign-ups) *(my call)*: Holiday, Sick leave, Training, Meeting (time away),
  Extra shift (extra time). Arabic names follow the language as other seeded lists do.
- A type in use can't be deleted; it can be made inactive (not offered for new entries, still shown).

### Availability entries

- **Planning › Availability**: a list of entries with filters (person, team, type, dates) and add / edit / delete. Also
  reachable from a person's row header on the board ("Add time away") *(my call)*.
- An entry: **type**, **a person or a team** (a team entry applies to whoever is a member on each day it is read), **from**
  and **to** dates (one day when the same), **days of the week** (empty = every day in the range, for repeating entries such
  as "every Sunday"), **hours per day** (empty = the whole working day; required for *Extra time*), notes.
- Rules: to ≥ from; a range of at most 366 days; hours more than 0 and at most 24; an active person or team; an active type.
- Edited with *Manage people* or *Edit work orders* (planners) *(my call)*; seen by everyone who sees the board.

### Hours available (one function, used everywhere)

For a person and a day: **pattern hours** (their working pattern for that weekday) **− time away** (an entry without hours
takes the whole day; entries add up; never below 0) **+ extra time**. A day off in the pattern with an extra shift has the
extra hours. The board's capacity, its red overload colour, the move warnings and the timesheet's "available" all use it.

### Planning board

- **Person rows**: each day cell shows the day's available hours (instead of the pattern's), and is tinted with the type's
  colour with its name when there is time away (a whole-day absence shows the type name across the cell). Overload stays
  red, now against available hours.
- **Warnings** (in the move answer, as today; never refused): a job dropped on, or a person added on, a day they are fully
  away — "Ali Hassan is away on that day (Holiday)."; a day that goes over their available hours — "This takes Ali Hassan to
  9 of 6 hours on 3 Dec." *(my call: the second replaces nothing; today the board only colours it)*.
- **Assign to me**: on cards in the Unassigned row, for a signed-in person linked to an employee with *Execute work orders*.
  It adds them to the work order (no planned hours) only while nobody is assigned; otherwise "Someone was assigned meanwhile.
  Refresh the board." Planners keep drag and drop.
- **Asset rows**: a card shows the expected stop from its PM task ("Stop ~4 h") next to the existing "only while stopped"
  mark.
- **By contractor** (third view beside person and asset): one row per company with visits in the range; a card is a contractor
  line on an active work order (work order number and title, company person, category, planned hours, hours done). Its day is
  the line's **visit date**, else the work order's day. Filters: site, company, category. Dragging to another day sets the
  visit date (*Edit work orders*); dropping on another company's row is refused with "Change the company on the work order."
  Clicking a card opens **Record the visit**: hours done, done on, person, notes (the 0.17.0 rules and permission
  *Execute work orders*), and a link to the work order. Lines without a day are in the undated tray.

### Contractor visit date

- `WorkOrderContractor` gains **visit date** (`PlannedOn`, optional). Set on the work order's Contractors tab and by the board;
  empty means "the work order's day". The phone shows it on the job's contractor lines *(my call)*.

### Daily timesheet (web)

- **Planning › Timesheet**: one person and one day (default: yourself, today).
  - Rows: work orders the person is on and whose day is that day, plus any work order they logged hours on that day; each
    with their hours, overtime and notes for that day — editable in place, added and deleted through the existing labour
    endpoints and rules (a closed work order stays read-only, as on its Labour tab).
  - "Add hours on another work order": a picker of active work orders.
  - Footer: the day's hours, overtime, and **available** hours with the availability entries that day.
  - Day arrows; a work order opens in its page.
- **Week view** (people with *Edit work orders* or *Log hours for others*): people × the seven days, each cell logged hours
  (overtime shown separately) against available hours, filters site, team and trade; a cell opens that person's day.
- A person without those permissions sees only their own timesheet; one not linked to an employee sees a message.
- Logging for someone else follows the web's labour rules as they are today *(my call: no new restriction)*.

### Daily timesheet (phone)

- **My day** in the technician app: a date (today, back 30 days as the phone's labour rule), the jobs from the phone's list
  that are mine and dated that day or carry my hours that day, my hours per job that day, and the day total against my
  available hours (sent with the bundle for the next 31 days and the last 30).
- **Add hours** per job uses the existing offline action `labor.add`. Editing and deleting entries stays on the web and the
  job's own screen *(my call)*.

### Permissions

No new permissions: types with *Manage settings*; entries with *Manage people* or *Edit work orders*; the board with *View
work orders*; moves with *Edit work orders*; "Assign to me" and visit reports with *Execute work orders*; the week view with
*Edit work orders* or *Log hours for others*.

### Data (one migration, `Availability`)

- New `AvailabilityTypes` (tenant, code unique per tenant, name, colour, kind, active; seeded) and `AvailabilityEntries`
  (tenant, type, person or team — exactly one, from, to, days of the week, hours, notes).
- `WorkOrderContractors` + `PlannedOn` (date, nullable).
- Every new text in Arabic.

## Review focus (most likely to bite)

1. Hours available: whole-day and part-day time away, overlapping entries, extra time on a day off, team entries after a
   member joins or leaves, repeating entries with days of the week, the person's own pattern vs their site's.
2. The board, the warnings and the timesheet agree on the same day's available hours.
3. The timesheet changes exactly the labour records the work order shows (no double count in costs).
4. Contractor drag changes only the visit date; recording a visit keeps the 0.17.0 rules.
5. Assign to me when two people press it at once.
