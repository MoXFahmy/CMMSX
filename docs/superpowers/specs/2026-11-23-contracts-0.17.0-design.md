# CMX 0.17.0 — Contracts and contractors

Roadmap release 0.17.0 (DimoMaint review, plan version 5), with the supplier catalogue split off into its own release.
Status: design for approval.

## Why

Lifts, chillers, fire systems and generators are usually serviced under contract. Missing a renewal or notice date costs
money, and contractor work is recorded today only when contractor technicians are set up as employees, at their own rate,
mixed into your own labour. DimoMaint has contracts (terms, amendments, periods, assets, alerts), partners with categories
and hourly costs, a named person on subcontracted resources (Q4 2026) and a contract report.

## Decisions taken with the user

- Contractor work is recorded as **contractor lines on the work order** (company, category and rate, hours, optional
  person) — the contractor needs no CMX login. Today's contractor employees keep working as they do.
- The **supplier catalogue** (several suppliers per part, purchase units) is **split off** into its own release right after.
- Everything else: "go with your picks" — the calls below marked *(my call)*.

## Goals

1. **Companies**: today's supplier records become companies that can be supplier, contractor and/or manufacturer, with
   several contacts and addresses; contractor categories with default hourly rates and per-company rates.
2. **Contractor lines on work orders**, costed into the work order and every cost report.
3. **Service contracts** with terms, automatic renewal, periods, amendments, covered assets, alerts before notice and end
   dates (bell + e-mail), links from work orders and PMs, and a contract report.

## Non-goals (0.17.0)

- Supplier catalogue per part and purchase units (next release).
- Contractor lines on the phone app.
- Cost centres and budgets (0.19.0).
- Import sheets for contracts, companies' contacts/addresses, categories or contractor lines.
- Contractor portal or logins for contractors; invoices and payment tracking.
- Changing the asset's existing warranty / service-contract field (it stays as it is).

## Anti-goals

- **Cost reports that double count** contractor work or silently drop it.
- **A renewal or notice date passing with no warning** while the alert is on.
- **Organizations that use none of this see changes**: no contracts, no contractor lines → screens and reports as before
  (a Contractor column showing zeros is fine).
- **Silent no-ops**: a refused action says why.

## Approaches considered

1. **A contract module on today's tables (chosen)**: extend the supplier table into the company record (roles, extra
   contacts and addresses); new tables for contracts, periods, amendments, covered assets, contract types, contractor
   categories and company rates, and a contractor-line table beside labour, parts and other costs. Cost reports gain
   contractor lines in the one place costs are summed (`ReportEndpoints.CostLinesAsync`).
2. **A new partners table replacing suppliers**: cleaner name, but rewrites purchase orders, the import and 0.16's requests
   to rename a table.
3. **Contractor lines as "other costs" with extra fields**: quick, but reports couldn't tell contractor time from invoices.

## What CMX has today (and keeps)

- `Supplier`: code, name, one contact (name, e-mail, phone), one address, tax number, payment terms, currency, an
  `IsContractor` tick, active. Purchase orders, requests and the import use it; its e-mail receives "Send to supplier".
- `Employee.IsContractor` + `Company` (text): contractor technicians assigned and logging labour like staff.
- `Asset.WarrantyType` (none / warranty / service contract), `WarrantyExpiresOn`, `WarrantyNotes`.
- Costs: labour (`WorkOrderLabor`), parts (stock issues/returns), other costs (`WorkOrderCost`); the work order's totals
  and planned totals; `CostLinesAsync` feeds Maintenance costs, the asset's Costs tab, the failure tree and asset work
  history; work groups roll up costs.
- Background services: `OverdueNotifier` (periodic, once per due date) is the model for contract alerts.

## Design

### Permissions

| Code | Name | Built-in roles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `contracts.view` | View contracts | Administrator, Maintenance Manager, Technician, Viewer |
| `contracts.manage` | Create and edit contracts | Administrator, Maintenance Manager |

Companies, categories and rates are managed with today's *Manage suppliers*; contractor lines with today's work order
permissions (below). The migration adds the new permissions to existing built-in roles of those names.

### Companies

- **Roles** on the supplier record: *Supplier*, *Contractor*, *Manufacturer* (any combination, at least one). Existing
  records: Supplier on; Contractor = today's tick.
- **Main contact and address stay** on the record as today (purchase orders and "Send to supplier" keep using them)
  *(my call)*; **extra contacts** (name, role, phone, e-mail) and **extra addresses** (label, address, city, country) are
  lists on the company.
- Purchasing › Suppliers becomes **Companies** with a role filter; purchase orders and requests pick only companies with the
  Supplier role, contractor lines and contracts only those with the Contractor role.
- **Contractor categories** (Settings › Contractor categories): code, name, default hourly rate (base currency), active.
- **Company rates**: a contractor company may have its own hourly rate per category (edited on the company).
- **Employees** gain an optional **company** (a Contractor-role company). Linked employees' logged hours count as contractor
  cost in reports (below). The old `Company` text stays and is shown when no company is linked.

### Contractor lines on work orders

New `WorkOrderContractor` rows on a work order (also on each child of a group as any work order):

- Company (Contractor role, active), category, person (a contact of the company, or free text), planned hours, hours done,
  date done, hourly rate, notes.
- **Rate**: filled from the company's rate for the category, else the category's default; editable on the line.
- **Who** *(my call)*: *Edit work orders* adds, edits or removes lines and planned hours; *Execute work orders* records
  hours done and the date (and the person) on existing lines. Closed or cancelled work can't change.
- **Cost** = hours done × rate, dated by the date done (today when not given). Planned cost = planned hours × rate.
- Work order totals gain **Contractor hours / cost**; planned totals gain contractor hours / cost; the job card print lists
  the lines.
- Work groups roll contractor cost up like the others.

### Cost reports

- `CostLine` gains a **Contractor** amount: contractor lines by their date done, plus labour logged by **employees linked to
  a company** (moved out of Labour, so nothing is counted twice).
- Every report built on it shows the new column or adds it to totals: Maintenance costs (+ Contractor column and chart
  series), the asset's Costs tab, the failure tree and asset work history totals.
- Maintenance costs by technician stays per employee (labour only), as today.

### Service contracts

**Record** `Contract` (organization-wide numbering `CON-00001`):

- Title, company (Contractor role), manager (a user), type (editable list **Contract types**, seeded Maintenance,
  Inspection, Lease, Warranty), trade (optional), site (optional; empty = organization-wide), notes, files.
- Start date, end date, value and currency, payment terms.
- Expected work orders: a number per year, quarter or month (optional).
- Terms: **automatic renewal** (on/off), renewal length in months, **notice period** in days, **alert days** (default 60).
- Covered assets with an optional yearly cost each.
- **Periods**: the first period is the start–end dates and value; each renewal adds a period. **Amendments**: date,
  description, optional new value (applies to the current period and later renewals).
- **Termination**: date and reason (manage permission); a terminated contract doesn't renew or alert.

**Status** (from dates, never stored): *Upcoming* (starts later), *Active*, *Expired* (past its end, not renewed),
*Terminated* (termination date reached).

**Automatic renewal**: a background service (with `OverdueNotifier`'s pattern: periodic, startup delay, `RunOnceAsync` for
tests) renews active contracts with automatic renewal on whose end date has passed: a new period from end + 1 day for the
renewal length, value from the latest amendment or period; repeated until the end is today or later.

**Alerts**:

- *Notice deadline* = end date − notice days (only when automatic renewal is on: it's the last day to stop it).
- *End alert* for contracts without automatic renewal: before the end date.
- In the alert window (alert days before the deadline or end, up to that date) the contract counts in the **bell**
  (*Contracts needing attention*, for people who may view contracts) and **one e-mail** goes to the manager and the
  organization's contract-alert recipients (Settings › Contracts) — once per deadline/end date (new switch
  `contract.alert`, on by default, personal opt-out applies).

**Links**:

- Work orders and PMs gain an optional **contract**; a PM passes it to the work orders it creates; the picker lists active
  and upcoming contracts, those covering the work's asset first.
- A request or work order whose asset is covered by an active contract shows "Covered by CON-00003 · Lift Co" (link).
- The asset page lists the contracts covering it.

**Contract report** (Reports › Contracts, `reports.view`): period (default this year) and site; per contract: company, type,
status, value of the periods overlapping the period, spend (all costs of work orders naming the contract, dated in the
period, from `CostLinesAsync`), work orders completed in the period, expected (per-period number scaled to the period).

### Screens

- **Contracts** menu entry under Maintenance (`contracts.view`): list (filters status, company, type, site, needing
  attention; export), contract form (`contracts.manage`), contract page (terms, alert dates, periods, amendments, covered
  assets, work orders naming it, files, terminate, add amendment).
- Purchasing › **Companies** (renamed tab): role filter, roles, extra contacts and addresses, category rates on the company.
- Settings: **Contractor categories** (with default rate), **Contract types**, **Contracts** section (alert recipients).
- People › employee form: company link.
- Work order: **Contractors** card (lines, planned and done, totals), contract field and "covered by" banner; PM schedule
  form: contract field; request drawer: "covered by" banner; asset page: contracts card.
- Bell; reports; notifications switch; Arabic for every new text.

### Data (one migration, `ContractsContractors`)

- `Suppliers` + `IsSupplier` (default true), `IsManufacturer`; new `SupplierContacts`, `SupplierAddresses`,
  `ContractorCategories`, `SupplierCategoryRates`; `Employees` + `CompanyId`.
- `WorkOrderContractors`; `WorkOrders` + `ContractId`; `PmSchedules` + `ContractId`.
- `Contracts`, `ContractPeriods`, `ContractAmendments`, `ContractAssets`, `ContractTypes`; tenant settings + contract-alert
  recipients; `NumberKind.Contract` (prefix `CON-`, organization-wide).
- Data: seed contract types for existing organizations; add the two permissions to built-in roles.

### Builder's questions, settled *(my calls)*

- **Currencies in the report**: the contract's value is shown in its own currency, spend in the base currency; no
  conversion (when they differ the columns say so).
- **Sites**: a contract with a site may cover only that site's assets and be named only on that site's work and PMs; an
  organization-wide contract may cover any asset. Covered assets at sites a person can't see are hidden from them.
- **Renewal dates**: a period of N months runs from its start to start + N months − 1 day (month-end dates clamp, e.g. a
  period starting 31 January for one month ends 28/29 February).
- **Alert recipients**: the manager plus the contract-alert recipients; when both are empty, everyone with *Create and edit
  contracts*.
- **Termination in the future**: alerts and renewal stop as soon as a termination date is set; the status turns Terminated
  on that date.
- **Removing a company's Contractor role, or deactivating it**: allowed; existing lines and contracts keep it, pickers stop
  offering it. Contractor categories and contract types in use can't be deleted (mark inactive instead).
- **Contractor lines on closed or cancelled work**: refused ("This work order is closed.").
- **Numbering**: `CON-` for the whole organization (no site prefix).

## Review focus (most likely to bite)

1. Cost reports with both a contractor line and a company-linked employee's labour on the same work order: each counted
   once, in Contractor; unlinked contractor employees stay in Labour.
2. Renewal across several missed periods (service down for months), a renewal on the 29th–31st (month lengths), and
   alerts not sent twice after a renewal or a restart.
3. Site access: a site-limited user sees organization-wide contracts and their own site's, never another site's; covered
   assets at other sites don't leak.
4. A company losing its Contractor role or going inactive while lines and contracts use it: existing records keep it,
   pickers stop offering it.
5. Rates: changing a category's or company's rate never changes hours already recorded (the rate is copied onto the line).
