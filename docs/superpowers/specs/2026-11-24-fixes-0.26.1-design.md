# 0.26.1 — Fix release (short design)

A fix release before 0.27.0 (company sign-in waits for the user's identity-provider setup). Of about 40 minor issues deferred by
the final reviews of 0.24.0, 0.25.0 and 0.26.0, the user picked the 21 that people would notice or that touch privacy or data
safety (2026-11-24, "Go with your picks"). Each item below says what a person should get. No new features, permissions or
settings; one patch release.

## Why

The three releases shipped with their reviewers' minor findings listed but not fixed. Some of them show wrong text or lists,
some let a site-limited person see other sites' data, and one can lead to duplicated assets after a crash. Fixing them now keeps
those from reaching customers who start on these features.

## Approaches

- **A. (picked)** One patch release with the 21 picked items, each test-first, one final review.
- B. Fix them inside 0.28.0. Rejected: mixes fixes with a large feature and delays them.
- C. Fix all ~40. Rejected: the rest are rare races with no data harm, test-only gaps, or behaviour the designs accepted.

## The fixes

### Serial-numbered parts (0.24.0)

1. **Serial and shelf-life don't mix.** A part can't be both serial-tracked and a shelf-life item: the part form and switching on
   serial tracking refuse it ("A serial-tracked part can't also be a shelf-life item."). Parts already both (if any) keep working as
   serial parts; their receipts and fits stop asking for a batch.
2. **A repair line is received whole.** Receiving a purchase order's repair line takes exactly its quantity or nothing ("Receive a
   repair line whole."); no half cost, no half-received line.
3. **The units list is complete or says it isn't.** Filters (state, store, site, ending soon, search, "not scrapped") apply before
   the 500-row cap, and the answer says when more than 500 match; the Units tab shows "Showing the first 500 units."
4. **Fitted units follow site limits.** An asset's "Fitted units" answer is refused (not found) when the person can't see the asset.
5. **Fitted units move with their asset.** Transferring an asset to another site moves the units fitted to it, whether or not its
   other child assets go too.
6. **No "then issue" on serial lines.** The purchase-order receive form doesn't say "then issue to WO-…" on serial lines (their
   units stay in the store until fitted).
7. **Fitting doesn't need View inventory.** The Fit dialog and the repair-line picker list units through answers open to whoever may
   do the action (execute work and move stock for fitting; create purchase orders for repair lines).

### Activity log and privacy (0.25.0)

8. **Anonymising reaches the e-mail log.** Settings › E-mail log rows sent to the person's old address (or name) show the placeholder
   address and name instead. Subjects and bodies are left as they were (they hold record titles, not the person's details).
9. **The log finds Arabic text and uses local days.** Searching the activity log matches Arabic (and other non-Latin) changed values,
   and the From/To dates are the organization's days in its time zone.
10. **Renewing a password is logged like signing in.** A wrong current password on the "choose a new password" step writes
    "Sign-in failed"; the try that locks the account writes "Locked out" (once).
11. **Anonymise acts on one person.** When an employee is named, any sign-in sent with it is ignored; only the employee's own linked
    sign-in is closed.
12. **Storage follows site limits.** A site-limited administrator's list of largest files shows only files of records they can see.
    The totals stay organization-wide (they show space, not records).
13. **The log's person filter works without Manage users.** It lists the organization's people and service accounts for anyone who
    may open the log.

### API keys and imports (0.26.0)

14. **An import's "done" is saved with the import.** The job is marked done, with its result, in the same save as the imported data.
    A crash right after can no longer show "Nothing was saved" for an import that saved.
15. **Service accounts aren't people, everywhere.** PM schedule e-mail recipients and a contract's manager refuse service accounts;
    the People page's "sign-in" picker doesn't list them.
16. **Service accounts can have branches.** The service account dialog has the same branch (location tree) field as a person.
17. **Keys can't read people's devices.** Every request to a person's devices is refused for keys ("API keys can't do this.").
18. **Removing old import files is honest.** An import's file is cleared from its history line only when the storage delete worked
    (or the file was already gone); otherwise it is tried again next time.
19. **A long error can't leave an import stuck.** Error messages are cut to fit before they're saved.
20. **The import history keeps up.** The history refreshes while any import is waiting or running; opening a finished import from
    the history no longer reloads every screen.

### Housekeeping

21. **Record names for the log are looked up within the organization** (defence in depth; no leak today).

## Left out (deferred again)

- Rare races with no data harm (phone vs web fit, quantity issue vs switch-on), duplicate store ids in a switch-on request (500).
- An open stock count with a part later made serial (cancel works); a work order's units "since" for registered units.
- English summaries of bulk changes; site settings logged as one object; "last changed" on other settings pages.
- Import edge cases the 0.26.0 design accepted (number gaps, collision fallback), restart e-mails, crafted-spreadsheet hardening.
- Test-only gaps; log volume on bulk runs.

## Constraints

- Version 0.26.1 (a fix raises the last number).
- Every new message in English and Arabic.
- No new permissions; no migration unless a fix needs one (none expected).

## Left to the builder

- Where each check lives, the exact answers' shapes (e.g. how the units list says it was cut), and the tests that pin each fix.
